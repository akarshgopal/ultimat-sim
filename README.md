**Attribution.** This codebase is derived from [Ben Schrauwen](https://github.com/benschrauwen)'s unofficial MIT-licensed [`terraform-simulator`](https://github.com/benschrauwen/terraform-simulator) ([terraform-simulator.com](https://terraform-simulator.com/)). It is an independent educational tool and is not affiliated with Terraform Industries.

An unofficial, MIT-licensed browser app for exploring coupled industrial processes. The main product is Molecular Foundry, a flowsheet workspace for connecting process blocks and inspecting material and energy flows.

The app is intentionally centered on the idea that very cheap local solar DC, low-capex intermittent hardware, and colocated conversion can matter more than chasing maximum utilization or maximum electrical efficiency.

## Current Foundry State

The root app now provides:

- A blank, editable process canvas with explicit material, electricity, heat, and consumable ports.
- Reusable desalination, electrolysis, DAC, Sabatier, ASU, Haber–Bosch, urea, MTO ethylene, brine-mineral, chlor-alkali, bromine, aluminium, MG-Si, Siemens-style polysilicon, PV module BOM assembly, ionic-clay REE leach, Bioforge gluconic, hydrogen-DRI steel, titanium, dry-process cement, copper SX-EW cathode, energy, storage, splitter, mixer, source, and destination blocks.
- A deterministic operating solver with capacity limits, bottleneck reporting, recycles, and visible balance residuals.
- A generic post-solve economics fold: source purchases, installed CAPEX, fixed and variable O&M, sale/disposal destinations, demand caps, replacements, delivered cost, NPV, and IRR. Screening **annual net cash** is capital-inclusive: `R − OPEX − annualized CAPEX` with `annualizedCapex = installedCapex × CRF(discountRate, projectLifeYears)`. NPV/IRR stay DCF (year 0 = `−installedCapex`; years 1..N = operating cash `R − OPEX` plus replacements — the annualized charge is not subtracted again).
- Editable economic assumptions on every source, converter, and destination, including zero-cost inputs for in-the-limit experiments.
- Captured-baseline scenario comparison and a synergy ledger for avoided purchases, avoided disposal, and added product revenue.
- Integrated methane + water-recycle, evidence-backed coastal methane, coastal methanol, and ammonia + brine industrial-hub examples. The coastal methane case binds Almería PVGIS solar, a cited Alboran multi-ion seawater assay (Millero/Pilson S=35 scaled to 36.5 g/kg; not a NaCl proxy), assumed intake, and unverified grid/freshwater/discharge to the plant, then dispatches a 24-hour typical day so night hours produce nothing unless a battery is assumed. The Mejillones methanol demo is a loadable electrolyzer + solid-sorbent DAC + SWRO + methanol plant on the Chile Atacama-coast preset (`mejillones-pvgis-2026-09-14`), with frozen PVGIS-ERA5 monthly yield (SARAH3 does not cover that longitude), the cited Atacama Pacific assay (Millero S=35 scaled 34.9/35), assumed intake, and unverified grid/freshwater/discharge. Synthesis electricity and methanol price stay screening. The Dead Sea hub cites the same PVGIS family, uses a frozen open-water ion assay, and marks freshwater and salt as assumed. Intake and discharge stay unverified inland. A literature assay is not a mineral concession. Minerals CAPEX uses the tea-screening DLE brine-throughput mid $12/(kg brine/day) (NREL OSTI 1782801 conversion; band $3–40). At that mid, Dead Sea demo scale is cash-positive because 4% fixed O&M tracks CAPEX (the old −$801k gate was the unsupported $80 OOM); high-band $40 stays cash-negative. Screening, not bankable. The abundance hub couples brine-derived bromide with chlor-alkali chlorine, then uses chlor-alkali hydrogen with ASU nitrogen for ammonia.
- Distinct DAC routes (solid-sorbent, liquid-solvent, electro-swing) with different heat and reagent contracts. Solid defaults sit in the IEA DAC 2022 S-DAC family with NASEM 2019 heat-dominated framing; liquid vectors cite Keith et al. 2018; electro-swing keeps Voskian cell-work electricity with screening capture/makeup. SWRO plant SEC 3.5 kWh/m³ / recovery 0.45 cites Elimelech & Phillip 2011 and Ghaffour 2013, with Voutchkov 2018 noted for best-in-class RO-train 2.5–2.8 kWh/m³. Sabatier ancillary 1 kWh/kg CH₄ stays screening in the Zapf/Baier band. Switching a route keeps compatible connections, does not rewrite an existing makeup chemical, and stays comparable against a captured baseline.
- Overview hero/metrics use `engine/uncertainty.js` quality (cited / recoverable / assumption / derived / screening) on cashflow, solar yield, land, and product rates: screening numbers use fewer significant figures, noisy screening chips stay off the dashboard, and a single not-bankable notice covers assumption-dominated **capital-inclusive** screening cash (R − OPEX − annualized CAPEX). Cited solar yield keeps the quality chip plus the PVGIS link and the monthly low–high band when a frozen monthly series is bound.
- A location bar: a Leaflet map picker plus coordinates. Click the map or Apply location to fetch or reuse PVGIS typical-day solar and bind it to the current factory. Live `seriescalc` is cross-origin and fails CORS on GitHub Pages; Apply location then uses a nearby frozen series from `data/pvgis-sites.js` (or the Almería hourly freeze) or the latitude screening band. Live hourly needs a same-origin proxy — none is bundled. Toggle OSM, real Global Solar Atlas annual GHI LERC tiles (`GSA_IRRAD` ImageServer, `sliceId=2`, cited; hourly PVGIS still fetched on Apply location), WRI Aqueduct Baseline Water Stress tiles (UMN SCOPE MapServer, `{z}/{y}/{x}`, cited, with a screening GridLayer fallback), `estimateFootprint` campus polygons, and network plant markers. Solar, water, and land colormaps are mutually exclusive. Land is a choropleth of agricultural land values in USD/ha (USDA NASS Land Values 2025 farm real estate by US state; Eurostat `apri_lprc` arable land by country including the UK 2018 row; Statistics Canada 32-10-0047-01 national farm land CAD/acre; ABARES/DAFF Farmland Price Indicator national broadacre AUD/ha; NCA Japan 令和7年 中田 yen/10a; Saudi MoJ 2023 agricultural-land deals — FX via documented ECB/Frankfurter rates or official SAR peg) plus labeled screening national/regional proxies (ODEPA-family Chile ads, Gujarat acre-order, Western Cape DoA sheets, GCC MoJ proxy for UAE/Qatar/Oman). Record quality is cited vs screening. Early siting proxy only, not cadastral or parcel comps; transparent where unpublished. If tiles or the map library fail, lat/lon still work. Site panels keep meteo cites, assay summaries, and rights chips (`authorized` / `assumed` / `unverified`) with a light `kind` (`grid` / `freshwater` / `intake` / `discharge` / `concession` / `purchase`). Unverified grid and freshwater stay explicit zeros until assigned (`authorize: false`); solve warns `unverified site right: KEY`. Size-to-target will not grow a resource whose right is unverified (`size-to-target cannot assume KEY`). Intake is not discharge; a concession is not a purchase.
- Cited inland/coastal **process-brine** assays (Abu Dhabi sabkha, Salar de Atacama Li, Lake Mackay WA, Great Salt Lake, Salton Sea / Imperial Valley geothermal, Uyuni, Qaidam, Danakil, Searles, Jizan Red Sea sabkha, Kutch subsoil, Texas Gulf desal-reject, Mediterranean SWRO reject) sit beside the Millero seawater files. Geography-matched coasts bind both: Gulf presets (Taweelah, Ras Laffan, **Ras Al-Khair**) get sabkha brine, Red Sea coasts (Oxagon/Ain Sokhna/Yanbu) get Jizan sabkha, Mejillones gets Atacama nucleus brine, Port Hedland/Kwinana get Lake Mackay playa brine, Mundra gets Kutch subsoil, Corpus Christi gets Texas desal-reject, Almería gets Mediterranean SWRO reject as WA salt-lake regional screening — seawater resources are not overwritten. `createAbundanceCase({ assayId, region })` (default `dead-sea-brine`, offtake `me-levant`) can run the minerals hub on those feeds; missing bromide is zero, not invented. Pass `region` from `site.region` (e.g. `Atacama/Chile`, `US West / California`) so Chile Li offtake is not the Dead Sea cap and Salton Sea inherits the existing texas/US demand table. A literature assay is not a mineral concession. Not an open-world GIS crawler.
- Discrete multi-site search (`searchAbundanceSites` / `node scripts/abundance-site-search.mjs`) scores the Dead Sea hub plus the preset catalog under the capital-inclusive cash gate. **Default path is materials** (`path: materials`, templates `abundance` only) so the maximizer never depends on fuels. Coastal methane and methanol live on a separate fuels-screening path (`searchFuelSites` / `--path fuels`). Abundance plants use each site's cited process-brine assay — not a Dead Sea chemistry clone — so Mejillones/Atacama Li, Gulf sabkha (including Ras Al-Khair), Lake Mackay, Port Hedland, Great Salt Lake, Salton Sea, Uyuni, Qaidam, Danakil, and Searles Lake are abundance-eligible (dual-assay coasts stay seawater-eligible too). Near-misses are the best **negative-cash operating** slates; idle (rate 0, no sale tonnes, cash≈0) does not rank. Default `rightsScenario` is `screening-assumes-intake-concession` (assumes intake/concession for evaluation only). `screening-assumes-intake-only` still assumes intake for coastal/methanol but does **not** assume a brine concession, so abundance plants skip/infeasible (`reason: no-concession`). `no-rights` does not assume intake or concession and skips/infeasibles plants that need them (`reason: no-rights`). `offtake-limited` keeps screening rights but tightens the **regional** demand caps (0.01×) so ranking/cash can change. `no-grid` overlays expensive islanded grid power ($0.50/kWh screening) so cash can drop. `freshwater-constrained` denies process-water rights (abundance `reason: no-freshwater`). `discharge-limited` denies seawater outfall (coastal/methanol `reason: no-discharge`). Not a permit. Screening TEA only: preset catalog, no real concessions. After cash>0 (and for near-misses), an optional `layerScore`/`softRank` from sun/water/land helpers may break ties when data exists — soft rank only, not a hard gate. Soft-rank sun prefers a site's frozen PVGIS daily/yearly yield when present (`pvgis-sites.js` / site-search `frozenSolarFor`); otherwise the latitude screening band. Missing land $/ha is omitted and the remaining layers are completeness-weighted (n/3) so sun+water are not silently over-weighted. Chile, Gulf (UAE/Saudi/Qatar/Oman), India, Japan, and South Africa now have land $/ha, so those regions are not completeness-penalized for missing land. The fuels path still runs a screening price×CAPEX `fuelProbe`; a fuel row would enter that path's cash+ ranking only at mid-band (tea-screening mid price × `capexFactor=1.0`). Mid-band cash is deeply negative on every frozen-PVGIS fuel coast (see the fuel-breakeven proof table); edge-of-band hits stay near-misses with `bestCash`/`breakEvenPrice`/`screeningPrice`/`capexFactor` and `selected.label=screening-edge`. When the winning slate is abundance minerals, `selected.family` is `abundance` (not a joint fuel tag). Map layers are not optimizer objectives.
- Location presets for real candidate coasts and brine hubs: Gulf (Taweelah, Ras Laffan, Ras Al-Khair dual-assay sabkha), Texas/US Gulf (Corpus Christi), India (Mundra), Australia (Pilbara Port Hedland, Kwinana, inland Lake Mackay), North Africa (Agadir, Dakhla), Atacama/Chile (Mejillones, inland Salar de Atacama), Red Sea (NEOM Oxagon, Ain Sokhna, Yanbu), Oman Duqm, Namibia Walvis Bay, Almería, inland Great Salt Lake, and inland **Salton Sea** (Imperial Valley / CA geothermal Li). - Location-aware site footprint: solar land from panel area ÷ GCR (latitude-adjusted row spacing, NREL/SAM-style), plus **cited or screening** process-pad intensities (PEM, DAC, Sabatier, methanol, SWRO/MED, brine, ASU, Haber–Bosch, battery; MSF and electro-swing DAC remain screening). The Footprint map layer draws separate solar + pad polygons in a deterministic campus layout (not one total circle). Network `landHa` sums those site totals, not a flat 1.6 ha/MWp.
- Co-product positive-cashflow sizing (`sizeForPositiveCashflow`): maximize the number of sale sinks with revenue while capital-inclusive `annualNetCash > 0` (revenue-proportional allocation of OPEX + annualized CAPEX makes every active sale product share that sign); ties break by higher net cash. Physics sizing first; dollars only after `evaluateEconomics`. Plants with both brine minerals and a fuel converter (Sabatier, electrolyzer, methanol, or ammonia) search abundance, fuel, and joint families rather than an abundance-only early return. Default search is a coarse scale/rate grid, then a bounded refine (geometric mid) once a sign-change or best-negative bracket exists. Tests keep a fast path (`scales`/`rates` of length 1, or `refine: false`). Size-to-target remains the single-product physics tool. Dead Sea abundance is a screening TEA: at the $12 DLE mid, demo scale (1e5 kg brine/day) is cash-positive because fixed O&M tracks CAPEX — not a bankable offtake, and high-band $40 stays cash-negative.
- Iterative size-to-target: pick CH₄, H₂, methanol, ammonia, lithium, salt, PV module, steel, ethylene, diesel, urea, titanium, float glass, cement, or copper and a kg/day rate. The outer loop sizes the loaded plant (desal, electrolyzer, DAC/Sabatier, methanol, ammonia, brine minerals, Mejillones PV-module chain, H₂-DRI steel, MTO ethylene, purchased-H₂ FT diesel, Walvis purchased-NH₃ urea, Mejillones purchased-TiCl₄ Kroll titanium, Mejillones purchased-sand/soda/limestone float glass, Mejillones purchased-limestone/clay cement, Mejillones purchased-PLS copper SX-EW, and PV as needed) until the operating solve is consistent. Every strategy with heat sinks/sources runs the T-feasible cascade inside duty estimation before buying residual heat or PV; leftover heat is screening electric resistance COP=1. The coastal demo remains a one-shot 37.5 kWp methane example until Size to target is used. Nuclear, solar-thermal, and thermal-storage stay out of the default More palette until TEA packs exist.
- A network rollup: multiple sited plants, product slate in t/year, land, optional haul corridors, and combined CAPEX/NPV. The fuels + minerals demo places solar methane at Almería, a brine/ammonia hub on the Dead Sea, the existing Mejillones silicon/PV plant, Walvis Bay green urea, Long Beach Maglut chromatography, and the existing Mejillones cement, copper SX-EW, float-glass, and green-FT plants. Corridors stay empty.

## Crustal silicon and aluminium (screening)

Metallurgical-grade silicon is a carbothermic SAF block (`mg-si`): SiO₂ + 2 C → Si + 2 CO at 12 kWh/kg electrical (mid of the 11–13 kWh/kg band). Carbon chemical energy is the C feed; the unit does not emit waste heat. Siemens-style poly upgrade (`polysilicon`) is MG-Si + power → solar-grade poly-Si at 65 kWh/kg (Fraunhofer ISE SoG 60–71 band mid; 1.05 mol feed / mol product). Hall–Héroult aluminium stays 14 kWh/kg. Screening PV module assembly (`pv-module`) wires poly-Si + purchased Ag paste + float glass + EVA + Al into a finished-module sale using Fraunhofer ISE 2021 mass shares on 11.6 kg/m² (remaining ~10% backsheet/J-box omitted). Ag is USGS MCS 2026 bullion $38/troy oz. Module ASP screening $0.15/W → $2.85/kg. The Mejillones demo is quartz → MG-Si → poly-Si + purchased Ag/glass/EVA + crustal bauxite → Bayer screening alumina (2.0 kg ore, 0.08 kg NaOH makeup, 3.5 kWh/kg total-energy-as-electricity proxy; not a full Bayer train) → Hall–Héroult Al → module on frozen PVGIS-ERA5 5.27 kWh/kWp·day; purchased solids are not a concession. Size-to-target can resize Mejillones PV module kg/day (baseline-ratio on that chain). Optional screening freight $/kg on binds: module sale and purchased Ag/EVA/caustic use chile-coast-container ($0.08/kg); bauxite, float glass, quartz, and carbon (reductant + anode) use bulk-dry-shortsea ($0.03/kg). Mejillones quartz/carbon short-sea + caustic container screening freight. Mejillones cement / Cu-EW / float-glass / FT(+green) leftovers reuse those same bands on purchased feeds and offtake. Not a logistics model. Not a cell fab, not a TCS plant model. Screening TEA, not bankable.

## Code map

- `index.html`, `flowsheet.css`, and `js/flowsheet-app.js`: editor, inspector, saved factories, and scenario comparison.
- `engine/model.js`: substances, streams, and graph validation.
- `engine/units.js`: process catalog and unit physics.
- `engine/solve.js`: operation, resource allocation, recycles, and balance diagnostics.
- `engine/heat.js`: post-solve temperature-feasible heat cascade (not HEN synthesis).
- `engine/economics.js`: costs and cash flows computed from the solved graph. Screening gate cash is `R − OPEX − annualized CAPEX`; NPV/IRR use upfront CAPEX + operating cash. Both economics panels use this result directly; IRR is a fractional rate.
- `data/tea-screening.js`: cited/screening TEA bindings. Optional screening freight $/kg (`freightBands` / `getFreight` / `bindSale`/`bindCost` `{ freight }`) is a plant-gate adder, not a routing model. Mejillones cement/Cu-EW/float-glass/FT(+green) leftovers bind chile-coast-container / bulk-dry-shortsea on purchased feeds and offtake. **Packs** (`packs[processId]`) hold `capexIntensity`, optional `capexIntensityBand` / `scaleExponent`/`refCapacity`, `fixedOmPercent`, `variableOm`, and evidence chips. Formula: `installedCapex = intensity × capacity × (capacity/ref)^(exponent−1)`, or `intensity × capacity` when the exponent is omitted. Minerals DLE intensity is a literature brine-throughput mid `$12/(kg brine/day)` with band `{low:3, mid:12, high:40}` (NREL TEA OSTI 1782801 / doi:10.2172/1782801 Table 3 Ventura IX ≈ $1.83 optimistic; same-table peer PEAs ≈ $4–15 converted the same way; multi-product Dead Sea hub ≠ Salton Sea Li-only DLE; screening OOM, not bankable). The old `$80` OOM is not supported by that conversion. Dead Sea demo scale is cash-positive at the $12 mid because 4% fixed O&M tracks CAPEX (even mid=20 stays cash+; high $40 is cash−) — not a drop to NREL $1.83. `bindCapexPack` / `bindCapex` apply that as `capexRate` (so CAPEX tracks sized capacity) except solar-PV, which precomputes `$/kWp`. **Demand caps** are regional offtake ceilings in kg/y (`demandByRegion` / `DEMAND_REGIONS`: `me-levant` default, `gulf`, `chile-atacama`, `australia`, `europe`, `india`, `texas`, `southern-africa`, `asia-china`, `default`) — not plant offtake contracts. `resolveDemandRegion` maps `SITE_PRESETS.region` strings (Atacama/Chile, Levant, Gulf, Europe, India, Texas/US Gulf, US West / California → texas, Southern Africa, Australia, China / Tibet and China / Qaidam → `asia-china`, …) onto those ids. `bindSale(key, { region })`, `bindSaleForRegion(region)(key)`, and `getDemandForRegion(region)` set `annualDemandLimit`. Chile Li stays a special brine-Li case: screening cap below USGS MCS 2025 Chile mine production 2024e ~49,000 t Li content — not the ME 1 kt cap, not `inherit:me-levant`, and not an offtake contract; other Chile minerals still inherit me-levant with `inherit:'me-levant'` on those rows. Australia / Texas / Southern Africa / India / Europe / Gulf / China-Asia mineral ceilings (Li, Br, potash, salt, gypsum, Mg-compound) use USGS MCS 2026 production or non-producer proxies instead of copying Dead Sea tables; Australia Li is hard-rock spodumene, not a Lake Mackay brine offtake. China / Tibet (Zabuye) and China / Qaidam use `asia-china`: conservative ceilings below USGS MCS 2026 China production (Li 2025e ~62,000 t content, Br ~90,000 t, potash ~6.3 Mt K₂O, salt ~56 Mt), not a silent ME-Levant inherit and not a plant contract. Fuels/chemicals on that table still inherit me-levant with `inherit:'me-levant'`. Fuels/chemicals (CH₄, methanol, ammonia, H₂, …) without a cited regional series inherit me-levant only with `inherit:'me-levant'` plus a note on each copied row — never a silent copy. `offtake-limited` haircuts those regional caps (0.01×). Screening industrial-power overlays via `bindCost('power', { region })` (IEA Electricity 2024/2026 family, plus EIA Texas industrial average for `texas`; not a PPA). Li `unitPrice` stays the USGS LCE proxy in every region; Chile has an explicit `priceByRegion` overlay (same $14/kg, supply-side note — not a LiCl contract). Regional CAPEX intensity multipliers (`capexMultiplierByRegion` / `getCapexMultiplierForRegion`) are screening labor/construction/EPC location proxies vs a US Gulf-ish baseline of 1.0 (India ~0.75, China ~0.80, Levant ~0.85, Gulf ~0.90, Southern Africa ~0.95, Texas 1.0, Chile ~1.05, Australia ~1.15, Europe ~1.20; unmapped `default` omitted → 1). Applied as `capexIntensity_region = pack.capexIntensity × multiplier[region]`. Turner & Townsend GCMI / World Bank ICP construction price levels / IRENA regional installed-cost family as direction; process equipment is traded so the construction spread is damped into ~0.7–1.3. Not plant quotes. NREL ATB is US-only. `solarCapexByRegion` may replace that damped factor for solar-pv only (India IRENA 2024 utility-PV TIC $525/kWp; not stacked with the 0.75 process-plant multiplier). `bindCapexPack` / `bindCapex` apply the factor (or solar overlay) to `capexIntensity` when `extra.region` is set. `createAbundanceCase({ region })` is backward compatible (omit = me-levant). Site-search passes `site.region` into abundance TEA and rebinds fuel-plant sales/power purchases. Fuel CH₄ $1/kg is a screening green-premium (EIA Henry Hub is contrast only); methanol $0.40/kg is a commodity-band mid.

Prices and CAPEX are screening, not quotes. `node scripts/fuel-breakeven.mjs` prints JSON plus a catalog midCash proof table. Native Almería/Mejillones cases keep their frozen series; overlays bind each site's frozen PVGIS from `data/pvgis-sites.js` (Port Hedland, Oxagon, Taweelah, …).

- `cases/`: runnable reference plants (`coastal.js`, `methanol.js`, `abundance.js`, `sabatier.js`, `network.js`, `silicon.js`, `float-glass.js`, `cement.js`, `cu-ew.js`, `h2-dri.js`, `green-h2-dri.js`, `ti-kroll.js`, `ree.js`, `bioforge.js`, `green-ammonia.js`, `urea.js`, `green-urea.js`, `mto.js`, `ft-liquids.js`, `green-mto.js`, `green-ft.js`).

## Model limits

The operating model uses representative-day flows and fixed installed capacities. It checks component/element, charge, electricity, and heat accounting, but does not provide full thermodynamic closure, hourly storage dispatch, or economic optimization. Automatic plant sizing is a separate outer loop (`engine/size.js`) that chooses capacities and `solarKWp` from a product target (CH₄, H₂, methanol, ammonia, lithium, salt, PV module, steel, ethylene, diesel, urea, titanium, or float-glass), then calls the same operating solver. Process and cost presets are editable screening assumptions. Foundry money and land use fewer significant figures when screening; Overview and Economics each keep one not-bankable / screening notice instead of repeating screening chips on every metric.

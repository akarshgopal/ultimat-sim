# CATALOG-LI-METAL — SKIP

Gate: electrolytic Li metal from LiCl/Li2CO3 only with open TIC→$/(kg/day) **and** SEC.

## Evidence reviewed
- **TIC (partial):** Li-Metal C2M concept study (Batteries News / company NR): brownfield 1,000 t/y carbonate-to-metal plant CAPEX USD 109M (−25/+50%). That is open plant TIC for proprietary C2M, not a peer island band for traditional molten LiCl–KCl electrolysis.

These SEC cites are for **chloride** electrowinning, not Li-Metal’s carbonate-to-metal C2M. C2M study publishes conversion OPEX ≤$31/kg, **not** SEC kWh/kg.

## Decision
